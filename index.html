The product thesis

Four pillars, one daily return.

MoodSignal is not a clinical diagnosis engine. It is a wellness-first pattern mirror: a simple daily ritual that helps users notice shifts, choose a supportive practice, and build a private record of what helps.

Listen

Voice mood diagnostics

The voice becomes a private daily signal.

Research links depression, stress, and emotional arousal to measurable speech changes, including pitch, speech rate, pauses, loudness, jitter, shimmer, spectral features, and language sentiment. MoodSignal should translate that research into cautious personal trend language.

Three recording modes give the engine context without making the user work.

Fixed reading prompt. Same paragraph each day for comparable pitch, pace, monotonicity, vocal energy, pause ratio, jitter, shimmer, and articulation.
Free speech prompt. A 30-60 second check-in such as "Talk about how you feel today" for natural language, hesitation, mood tone, and sentiment.
Guided emotional prompt. Questions like "What do you need most today?" help Alicia recommend a practice without pretending to diagnose.
Personal baseline. The most useful question is not "Does this person sound like a dataset?" It is "Does this person sound different from their own usual pattern?"

Output language

Use human categories: mood tone, energy, calmness, stress signal, speech stability, and baseline shift.

Quality checks

Flag background noise, clipping, low volume, speech duration, silence ratio, and microphone consistency.

Research posture

Voice biomarkers are promising, especially for depression monitoring, but the site should always say validation matters.

Breath meditation

Ancient attention, modern nervous-system support.

This section can mention Anapana as an ancient breath-awareness technique while keeping the app language accessible. The practice is intentionally minimal: sit still, feel the breath, notice wandering, return.

15Quick reset for stress, transition moments, and emotional interruption.
30Daily balance session with enough time for the mind to settle.
45Deep practice for emotional regulation and awareness training.

The research supports breathwork as a promising stress and mental-health support, while also warning against hype. MoodSignal should keep the claim precise: supportive practice, not cure.

Perfect Stillness

Quiet mental chatter by listening to the body.

Perfect Stillness is the accessible product name for body-based awareness practice. It is inspired by body-scan and insight-meditation traditions: move attention through the body, feel what is actually there, release judgment, and move on.

The practice script

Start at the top of the head. Rest attention on one area until sensation is noticed: pressure, warmth, tingling, pulsing, numbness, or nothing at all. Once something is known clearly, move to the next area. The goal is not drama. The goal is contact with reality as it is.

No chasing pleasant sensations.
No fighting unpleasant sensations.
No story needed, just observation.

Why it belongs in MoodSignal

Voice analysis gives a signal. Perfect Stillness gives a response. The practice trains users to notice sensations before turning them into identity, narrative, or reaction.

Frequency-assisted relaxation

Sound as support, not spectacle.

MoodSignal can include binaural beats, isochronic tones, ambient music, sleep soundscapes, and breath-and-tone sessions. The evidence language should stay careful: these are relaxation aids, not medical claims.

Headphones or speakers?

Binaural beats require separate left and right channels, so they belong in headphone mode. Speaker mode should use ambient music, isochronic pulses, nature textures, or gentle drones.

Headphones: binaural beat sessions for calm, focus, rest, and meditation.
Speakers: ambient, isochronic, rain, low drones, soft bells, and guided breath.

Technical blueprint

Build the app as a privacy-aware mobile system.

The website should show that the idea is not just beautiful. It is technically buildable with React Native, Expo, Firebase, careful audio capture, optional ML services, and a realistic validation roadmap.

Mobile app

  • React Native + Expo for iOS and Android velocity.
  • Expo audio recording, push notifications, and local session timers.
  • Firebase Auth with Google sign-in if accounts are needed.
  • Firestore for user profile, check-ins, practice history, and daily content state.
  • Encrypted storage path for audio, with a delete-after-feature-extraction option.

Voice and ML pipeline

  • Audio quality gate: duration, noise, clipping, volume, and voice activity.
  • Acoustic extraction with openSMILE, librosa, Praat/Parselmouth, or equivalent services.
  • Optional transcription with Whisper for sentiment and reflection analysis.
  • Model path: baseline rules, then XGBoost/LightGBM, then speech embeddings such as wav2vec 2.0 or WavLM.
  • Clinical-grade layer later: consent, validated scales, blinded labels, longitudinal study design.

Validated screeners

PHQ-9 and GAD-7 can be optional self-report screeners, clearly labeled as validated tools rather than MoodSignal diagnoses.

Safety posture

Use crisis-aware language, clear escalation pathways, and reminders that severe symptoms deserve professional support.

Privacy posture

Default to minimal data, user deletion controls, consent-based research use, and local-first analysis where feasible.

Research library

Every claim gets a source and a boundary.

The science layer should make the page credible without pretending the evidence is stronger than it is. Each citation below connects directly to a MoodSignal feature and states why it matters.

Voice mood signal

  • Smartphone voice scoping review: supports phone-based voice data as a promising depression monitoring input.
  • Automatic speech meta-analysis: frames ASA as an emerging objective depression biomarker area.
  • BMC Psychiatry 2024: links acoustic and linguistic speech features with MDD symptom patterns.
  • Voice stress biomarker review: supports fundamental frequency as a stress-related voice signal.

Breath and stillness

  • Breathwork meta-analysis: finds breathwork associated with lower stress, anxiety, and depressive symptoms, with caution.
  • JAMA mindfulness meta-analysis: supports mindfulness programs for psychological stress and well-being outcomes.
  • Body scan meta-analysis: useful for framing Perfect Stillness while acknowledging mixed effects for body scan alone.
  • Vipassana evidence review: background support for insight-style awareness practices without using that label in product copy.

Reflection and mobile support

  • Self-affirmation review: supports values-based affirmation as a low-friction psychological intervention.
  • Gratitude meta-analysis: supports gratitude prompts as one evidence-informed reflection format.
  • Positive psychology meta-analysis: gives broader context for positive activity interventions.
  • Binaural beats review: supports careful sound-session language.
  • EMA well-being review: supports phone-based momentary mood and well-being measurement.
  • Ecological momentary interventions: supports just-in-time app interventions.
  • Mobile mental-health meta-review: supports potential while emphasizing evidence limits.
  • PHQ-9 validation and GAD-7 validation: optional validated self-report screeners.

Scientific citations on this website support the product rationale. They do not mean MoodSignal has been clinically validated. Any future clinical claims should require a formal validation study, careful safety review, and appropriate regulatory guidance.
