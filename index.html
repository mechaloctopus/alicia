Voice mood diagnostics

The voice becomes a private daily signal.

Speech carries measurable signals: pitch, pace, pauses, loudness, jitter, shimmer, spectral features, and language tone. MoodSignal translates those signals into personal trends.

Three recording modes keep the signal consistent and personal.

Fixed reading. Same passage each day for comparable pitch, pace, energy, pause ratio, jitter, shimmer, and articulation.
Free speech. A 30-60 second check-in for language tone, hesitation, rhythm, and emotional content.
Guided prompt. Questions like "What do you need most today?" connect the signal to the next practice.
Personal baseline. The strongest signal is the user's own change over time.

Output language

Mood tone, energy, calmness, stress signal, speech stability, and baseline shift.

Quality checks

Noise, clipping, volume, speech duration, silence ratio, and microphone consistency.

Perfect Stillness

Quiet mental chatter by listening to the body.

Perfect Stillness is body-based awareness: move attention through the body, feel what is present, release judgment, and continue.

The practice script

Start at the head. Rest attention on one area until sensation is clear: pressure, warmth, tingling, pulsing, numbness, or quiet. Then move on. The practice is direct contact with reality as it is.

Let pleasant sensations pass.
Let unpleasant sensations pass.
Stay with observation before story.

Why it belongs in MoodSignal

Voice analysis gives the signal. Perfect Stillness gives the response: notice sensation before it becomes identity, narrative, or reaction.

Sleep and screen time

Protect the night so the mind can repair.

MoodSignal should track sleep windows, screen curfews, bedroom light, and morning recovery. The guidance stays simple: darker room, fewer late screens, steadier wake time, and no glowing devices beside the bed.

Dark bedroom protocol

Cover LEDs, remove glowing chargers, close curtains, charge the phone outside the bedroom, and use an alarm clock that does not light the room.

Frequency-assisted relaxation

Sound for calm, focus, and rest.

MoodSignal can include binaural beats, isochronic tones, ambient music, sleep soundscapes, and breath-and-tone sessions. Each mode has a clear use case and a measured claim.

Headphones or speakers?

Binaural beats require separate left and right channels. Headphone mode handles binaural tracks. Speaker mode uses ambient music, isochronic pulses, nature textures, and gentle drones.

Headphones: binaural beat sessions for calm, focus, rest, and meditation.
Speakers: ambient, isochronic, rain, low drones, soft bells, and guided breath.

Technical blueprint

Build the app as a privacy-aware mobile system.

The product is technically straightforward: Expo mobile client, Firebase identity and data, encrypted audio handling, saved goals, lifestyle logs, a Python signal pipeline, and a validation roadmap.

Mobile client specs

  • React Native + Expo, TypeScript, Expo Router, EAS Build, iOS and Android.
  • Audio capture target: mono WAV/PCM, 16 kHz minimum, 30-90 second samples.
  • Expo Notifications for daily prompts, practice reminders, and streak recovery.
  • Local session timers for 15, 30, 45, and 60 minute breath or stillness sits.
  • Firebase Auth with Google sign-in; anonymous mode for privacy-first onboarding.
  • Firestore records: profile, baseline, check-ins, goals, fasting logs, sleep windows, screen curfews, practice sessions, daily content, consent state.
  • Goal schema: title, reason, category, cadence, next action, reminder, recovery rule, linked mood tags, active state.
  • Lifestyle schema: fasting date, hydration, energy, sleep, screen curfew, bedroom darkness, morning recovery.

Voice pipeline specs

  • Quality gate: noise floor, clipping, RMS volume, speech duration, silence ratio, voice activity.
  • Acoustic features: F0, pitch variability, speech rate, pause ratio, energy, jitter, shimmer, MFCCs.
  • Optional transcription with Whisper for sentiment, self-reference, rumination, and gratitude markers.
  • Model path: baseline deltas, calibrated rules, XGBoost/LightGBM, then wav2vec 2.0 or WavLM embeddings.
  • Storage policy: extract features, encrypt audio, allow automatic audio deletion after processing.
  • Validation path: PHQ-9/GAD-7 labels, consented longitudinal data, bias checks, blinded evaluation.

Validated screeners

PHQ-9 and GAD-7 can run as optional self-report screeners with dated scores, severity bands, and escalation copy.

Safety posture

Include crisis-aware language, grounding tools, trusted-contact prompts, and professional support links.

Privacy posture

Use minimal data, deletion controls, consent-based research, and local-first analysis where feasible.
